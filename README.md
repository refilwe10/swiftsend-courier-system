A web-based courier management system for SwiftSend Couriers, designed to streamline delivery operations, improve customer communication, and reduce reliance on manual processes.

---

## 📋 Project Overview

SwiftSend Couriers is a Johannesburg-based courier company that currently relies on manual methods such as phone calls, paper-based forms, and spreadsheets to manage customers and deliveries. These processes make it difficult to maintain accurate records, track deliveries efficiently, and provide customers with timely delivery information.

**The SwiftSend Courier Management System** provides:
- A **customer website** for online booking and parcel tracking
- An **administrator dashboard** for managing customers, drivers, and deliveries
- A **driver mobile application** (planned) for updating delivery status

## Features

### For Customers
- User registration and secure login
- Online delivery booking with pickup and delivery details
- Parcel tracking using tracking number
- Real-time delivery notifications
- View delivery history

### For Administrators
- Dashboard with system overview
- Customer management (view, add, edit, delete)
- Driver management (view, add, edit, delete)
- Delivery assignment and management
- Generate delivery and driver efficiency reports
- Monitor overall delivery process

### For Drivers (Mobile App - Planned)
- Mobile login
- View assigned deliveries
- Update delivery status (picked up, in transit, delivered)
- Proof of delivery capture
